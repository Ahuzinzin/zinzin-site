Reign of Poseidon

6×4 grid · Pay Anywhere · Tumbling Reels · RTP 96.0% · Max win 5,000x

Pay Anywhere

8 or more matching symbols anywhere on the grid pay. Payouts are shown as multiples of your total bet.

Tumbling Reels

Winning symbols shatter and new ones fall into their place. Tumbles continue as long as new wins appear.

Poseidon's Lightning

Pearl multipliers from 2x up to 100x can drop onto the grid. When a tumble sequence ends with a win, Poseidon strikes every pearl and their values are added together and applied to the total win.

Free Spins

4 or more SCATTER symbols award 10 free spins. During free spins every pearl multiplier that lands on a winning tumble sequence is added to a total multiplier that stays for the whole feature. 3 or more SCATTER symbols during the feature award 5 more spins.

Buy Feature

Free spins can be bought instantly for 100x the current bet.
