How to play

  1. Choose your bet and press Spin. Every spin costs one bet; your progress on the rings carries over until you cash out.
  2. Every spin the orb reveals a symbol. Zeus, Poseidon or Hades advance their ring by one sector. Wind blows the spin away — nothing happens and the spin's bet is lost.
  3. Your win is your bet × the sum of every ring's current multiplier.
  4. Fill a ring completely to instantly bank its bonus (+20x / +10x / +5x) — the ring then starts over.
  5. The rare Cracked Urn shatters the round: all unbanked winnings are lost.
  6. Cashout takes everything. Part Cashout (2+ sectors in a ring) banks half and halves your progress, so you can keep playing.
